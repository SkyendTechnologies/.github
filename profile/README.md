## About

**Skyend Technologies** is an engineering-focused technology organization exploring systems where security, verifiability, resilience, and practical product design matter from the beginning.

Our work spans research, experimentation, and production-oriented engineering with an emphasis on clear architecture and systems that remain understandable as they evolve.

## Focus

- **Security engineering** — explicit trust boundaries, defensive architecture, secure defaults, and auditable behavior.
- **Applied cryptography** — data protection, cryptographic design, key-management concepts, and verifiable state.
- **Distributed systems** — decentralized architectures, synchronization, resilience, consistency, and realtime coordination.
- **Applied AI** — intelligent automation, analysis, experimentation, and AI-assisted engineering workflows.

## Principles

**Security by design** · **Verifiability** · **Modularity** · **Observability**
