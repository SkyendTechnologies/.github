# Code of Conduct

Skyend Technologies aims to keep technical collaboration productive, respectful, and focused.

## Expected behavior

Contributors and maintainers should:

- communicate clearly and respectfully;
- critique ideas and implementations rather than people;
- provide technical context for disagreements;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, and project boundaries;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, or deliberately disruptive behavior;
- assume good intent while still addressing concrete problems directly.

## Technical discussion

Strong disagreement is acceptable. Personal attacks are not.

Reviews should focus on correctness, security, maintainability, usability, architecture, evidence, and project requirements.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when behavior repeatedly disrupts the project or violates these expectations.

Serious concerns can be raised privately through:

https://t.me/SkyendTechnologies

Repository-specific conduct rules, where present, take precedence.
